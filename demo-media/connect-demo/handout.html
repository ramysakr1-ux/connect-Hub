Time twins

Two women born on the same day, in two different countries. Here are the words for today’s lesson, so you can look at them before you come.

Five words

shynervous with people you don’t know
got marriedbecame husband and wife
a companya business that makes or sells something
a fashion photographera person whose job is taking photos of clothes and models
a professional modela person whose job is wearing clothes for photos

In class

  1. Look at the two photos. Why are they “time twins”? What can you guess about their lives?
  2. Read one woman’s story. Put the six paragraphs in order. Then tell your partner her story in four or five sentences.
  3. Now read both stories. Find three things the two women have in common.

Talk about it

  1. Whose life would you like to have? Why?
  2. Find three things you have in common with one of the women. “I’m shy too, but I love sport.”
