When in Rome …

The tasks for Text C, for the class in the brief.

Appendix 2 · Reading for gist

Two minutes. Read fast. Which sentence is the writer’s main message?

  1. An old story explains why people in Milan stopped eating on Saturdays.
  2. An old expression tells us to follow the customs of the place we are in.
  3. Travelers should always learn the history of the countries they visit.

Appendix 3 · Reading for detail

Part 1. Read the text again. Put the six events in order, 1 to 6.

  1. Saint Ambrose told him what he did.
  2. He moved from Rome to Milan.
  3. The expression was born.
  4. He fasted on Saturdays in Rome.
  5. He asked for advice.
  6. He noticed that people in Milan did not fast on Saturdays.

Part 2. True or false? Show your partner the line in the text.

  1. The expression is only about visiting a new country. T / F
  2. Saint Ambrose did the same as the people of the city he was in. T / F
  3. You can use the expression about a new place like a library. T / F

Appendix 4 · Speaking: When in my country …

Write three tips for a visitor to your country: greetings, eating, public transport, or your own idea. Then tell your group. Ask one question about each tip you hear.
