4Read quickly. Did the date go well?

Read the dialogue in under a minute. Don't worry about every word. Circle one.

Good dateBad date

Sarah and Priya

Sarah: Hey Priya, guess what? I went on a date last night!

Priya: Really? That's exciting! How did it go?

Sarah: It was really nice! We went to a nice café.

Priya: Oh, nice! What did he look like?

Sarah: Well, he's tall with brown hair and green eyes. And he has a beard.

Priya: Sounds nice! What did you do at the café?

Sarah: We talked a lot and had coffee. He's really funny and easy to talk to.

Priya: That sounds great! Did you like him?

Sarah: Yeah, I think so! We're planning to go out again next week. I need to think of what we will do!

Priya: That's wonderful, Sarah! I'm happy for you.

Sarah: Thanks, Priya! It was fun.

8Read the second dialogue and answer

Sarah and Jack

Sarah: Hey, Jack! I had a great time yesterday.

Jack: Me too, Sarah! I really enjoyed talking to you.

Sarah: I'm happy to hear that! I was thinking, how about we go out again next Saturday afternoon? There's a new romantic comedy playing at the cinema, and I thought it would be fun to see it together. The movie starts at 3 PM.

Jack: That sounds like a good idea! What's the movie called?

Sarah: It's called Love Actually. I heard it's really sweet and funny. We could eat something before the movie too. There's a nice Italian restaurant near the cinema. How about we meet there at 2 PM?

Jack: Italian sounds great! 2 PM next Saturday works for me. I'll be there.

  1. Why did Sarah want to go to the movies for their next date?
  2. What else could Sarah suggest for their date?
  3. What time does the movie start?
  4. What kind of food do you think is at the Italian restaurant?
  5. What do you think of Sarah's date idea?
  6. What would be your ideal date?
