Lesson Plan

Main Aims

What the learners will be able to do by the end. Usually: To clarify and provide controlled practice of… / For learners to practise reading for gist and detail… Use bullet points, one per line.

Subsidiary Aims

What else the lesson develops along the way — a skill, or lexis that supports the main aim. Use bullet points, one per line.

Personal Aims

Take these from the action points in your last feedback, and say what you will actually do about each one. Use bullet points, one per line.

Loads the starred points from your last returned feedback, saved in this browser. Your plan is not affected.

Anticipated Problems & Solutions

Problems for learners with tasks and materials, technology, classroom management, interaction patterns, etc. (NOT language)

Class Profile

Who you are teaching: how many, nationalities and first languages, what they are good at and less good at, and what the group has covered recently. Two or three lines is enough — it is here so your aims and materials can be judged against the actual group.

Materials

Everything you and the learners will need, including where it came from. Use bullet points, one per line.
