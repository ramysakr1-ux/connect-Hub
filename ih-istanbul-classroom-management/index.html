Your turn: rescue this teacher Rewrite it
“Well, what I'm gonna do is I'm gonna ask you to get into pairs, but before that there are some things we've gotta work out. So just jot down if you've got a pen, could you write this, then when we've finished that we're going to do the next thing which involves…”

The task: learners read five questions, then ask a partner and write down the answers. Write the instructions in short steps, and add one question to check they've understood.

  1. “Work with your partner.” (gesture: you two, you two)
  2. “Read the five questions. Don't write.” (hold up the handout)
  3. “Ask your partner the questions. Write their answers.”
  4. “Five minutes.” (show five fingers)
Check: “Are you writing your answers, or your partner's?” (My partner's.) Then demonstrate the first question with one student before everyone starts.
