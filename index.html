No login, no server

Exchange with your tutor

Your tutor is on their own browser, so nothing here reaches them on its own. Export a file and send it to them (email, chat, however you'd share a document) once you've turned things in. When they return marked work, import the file they send back. If your centre has reworked the assignments, they will send you a wording file too — import it here and the assignment forms use it.

The order matters. Write your self-evaluation before you read your tutor's feedback — that is the point of it. Everything above saves itself as you go and carries automatically from one form to the next in this browser — turning in just tells your tutor it's ready.
