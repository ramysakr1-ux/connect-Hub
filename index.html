Trainee

Your CELTA paperwork

Your teaching practice and your written assignments, in the order you need them. Each form saves itself as you go — nothing reaches your tutor until you turn it in.

Timetable

    Teaching practice

    Lesson plan & language analysis

    Aims, class profile, anticipated problems, materials and the procedure, with the analysis sheet underneath. Choose a lesson shape and the stages fill themselves in.

    Self-evaluation

    What went to plan, what didn't, and what you did about last TP's action points. Write it before you read your tutor's feedback — that is the point of it.

    Teaching practice feedback

    Your tutor's strengths, action points and grade, with their comments on your plan and self-evaluation. Every teaching practice they have returned stays here, and the lot prints as one PDF to keep.

    Written assignments

    The four written assignments, in your centre's order, with the marking criteria each one is read against. They run alongside your teaching practice.

    Observation tasks

    Watching other people teach — your own group, filmed lessons and live ones — and writing up what you saw. The filmed and live write-ups go in your portfolio.

    TP7 & TP8 planning

    The last two teaching practices are yours to plan: a main aim, a sub aim and the material for each, on one grid your whole group can see.

    Candidate agreement

    What your centre asks of you and what you may ask of it — attendance, plagiarism, complaints and resubmissions. Read it and sign it.

    TP points

    What you are teaching at each TP. The lesson type rotates round your group so everybody covers the full range.

    CELTA 5

    Your record for the whole course — the two confirmations at the start, the stage records you and your tutor each write your half of, and the tables that fill themselves from your work.

    Your progress

    Every teaching practice and every assignment on one grid, read from what your tutor has returned to you. The same view your tutor has of you.

    Read your feedback, then start the next teaching practice from there — that files this one's plan, self-evaluation and feedback away and opens a fresh set.
