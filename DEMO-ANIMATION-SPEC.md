# Connect Lite — the animation

**Spec for the animated walkthrough. 26 September 2026, rewritten 27 September as a journey.**

Audience: **trainers and centre owners**, not trainees. Length: **4½–5 minutes**. One voice throughout — a trainer's, present tense, plain, first person plural where it fits ("we send one link"). Nobody is introduced and nobody's name is zoomed on: the film is about a course, not about who built the tool. The foot of every screen says *designed and built by Ramy* and that is enough.

Every scene names the screen, what the cursor does, what the voice says, and where the picture slows. ◆ marks a **hold** — a selling point where the picture stays a beat longer than the eye needs and the cursor stops. Zoom is written where it matters; everything else is a plain screen at 1280 px.

**The shape of the film is a journey.** One link is sent. The trainer sets the course up. A trainee does a teaching practice. The trainer gives feedback. The trainee reads it and does the written work. The assessor comes. The course ends with its reports. Start to finish, in the order it happens.

## Two demo courses, and which scenes come from which

Lite lives at **`https://lite.celtaconnect.com/`** — every link in the film shows that address, never `github.io`. Both courses are in the owner console:

- **`c3` — running.** Elmswood English Centre, twelve candidates, three weeks in: TP1–3 written up and returned, the fourth in progress. Tutors Jordan Blake and Diane Okonkwo; Emily Carter for the trainee side. **Parts 1–5** come from here — everything that shows a course *in motion*.
- **`c4` — finished.** *CELTA — C/16 2026*, the same centre, twelve candidates, eight TPs each, four assignments each, a full end-of-course report for every one. **Parts 6–7** come from here — the assessor pack, the grades, the final report — because a course three weeks in has none of those yet, and a report that names TP7 on a course at TP3 is what an assessor notices. **Nothing in the film may show a finished document on the running course.**

Capture from the live links, not from mock-ups. The tutor link opens everything. Never paste a link into this file — the repo is public and a link is a key.

## The three things the film must establish

1. **One link, and a course happens inside it.** No accounts, no passwords, no installing. The link opens anywhere a link opens and keeps working when the internet drops.
2. **Nothing is typed twice.** The plan becomes the feedback sheet; the feedback becomes the grades; the grades become the assessor's pack and the final report. Show the same words travelling, not features.
3. **It's paper when you need paper.** Every document prints as it stands.

## Part 3 · The trainee's teaching practice — 1:10–2:25

### 6 · The trainee's home — 1:10–1:20
**Screen:** `index.html` — the **hero card**: one lifted card headed *Teaching practice*, and inside it three panels — *Lesson plan & language analysis*, *Self-evaluation*, *Teaching practice feedback* — each a card of its own. Exactly one is **gold**: the work that is theirs now, *Finish it and turn it in* beneath it. When the work is with the tutor instead, the card's head carries a **teal** chip — *With your tutor* — and no panel is gold. Below the card, the written assignments.
**Voice:** "This is what a trainee sees. One card, and inside it the three things a teaching practice is. One of them is gold — the next step. Nothing else is asking for anything."
◆ Hold on the single gold panel. Zoom so the panels-inside-a-card reads.

### 7 · The plan, written and spoken — 1:20–1:50
**Screen:** `1_trainee_plan_and_analysis.html`.
**Cursor:** click into *Main aim*; the **Dictate** button in the bar — its dot goes red, the label reads *Stop dictating* — and the aim writes itself as the trainee speaks. *Stop dictating*. Then the **Lesson shape** menu → *Receptive skills* → six stages appear on the spine. Aims written into the stages (typed, fast-forward), minutes stepped with − and +, interaction chips picked. The **time budget** fills to *45 of 45 · fits exactly*.
**Voice:** "Click into a box and talk — the plan takes dictation. A lesson shape names the stages; the aims are the trainee's to write, or to say — the framework never writes those for them. Minutes and interaction sit with each stage, and the budget bar says when the lesson is full."
◆ Hold on the words arriving as they are spoken. ◆ Hold on the shape pick → the stages appearing. ◆ Hold on *fits exactly*.
*Dictation is cursor-in-the-box and talk. Nothing is copied or pasted. It is in three places — the plan, the self-evaluation, the tutor's feedback — and it is Chrome or Edge on a computer only (build notes).*

### 8 · Materials from Drive — 1:50–2:00
**Screen:** same page, the Materials card.
**Cursor:** a Drive share link pasted into *Materials link* → *Drive file attached* appears with the preview under it.
**Voice:** "Worksheets live on the trainee's own Drive. Paste the link and it is attached — the tutor opens it straight from the plan, and nothing is uploaded anywhere."
◆ Hold on the preview appearing. Zoom on *Drive file attached*.

### 9 · The analysis sheet — 2:00–2:15
**Screen:** Language Analysis opened, empty.
**Cursor:** click the **type** box → the menu opens and shows all three — *Functional language · Grammar · Vocabulary* — a beat, then *Vocabulary*. Four items: meaning, form, pronunciation, the problems and the solutions. Click into a transcription → the **phonemic chart** appears → a symbol lands in the box.
**Voice:** "The language analysis is part of the same document — functions, grammar or vocabulary. Meaning, form, pronunciation, with the problems they anticipate and what they will do about them. The phonemic chart is there when the transcription needs it."
◆ Hold on the open menu showing the three. ◆ Hold on the chart, and the symbol landing.

### 10 · Turning in, and the self-evaluation — 2:15–2:25
**Screen:** *Turn in* → the confirm. Cut to `2_trainee_self_evaluation.html` after the lesson: four boxes full, the fifth being **dictated** — the red dot, the sentence arriving.
**Voice:** "Turn in, and the tutor has it — no file, no email. After the lesson, before the feedback: what went to plan, what didn't, what they would change — said out loud on the way home, if that is easier than typing it. Written *before* they read the trainer. That is the point of it."

### 12 · Feedback, said — 2:35–2:55
**Screen:** `3_tutor_feedback.html` for Zeynep Aydın (TP3, ready for feedback). *What they planned*: the spine, read-only, the trainer's comment column beside it.
**Cursor:** click into *Strengths in teaching*, **Dictate**, and a point arrives as the trainer says it. *This is dictation: cursor, talk, done. No clipboard. The next scene is a different thing.*

### 13 · Feedback, all at once — 2:55–3:15
**Screen:** the same sheet, the **exchange** card.
**Cursor:** *Copy* → cut to a dictation window (any model; the brief pasted, the trainer talking through the lesson) → back → *Paste something back* → **every box fills**: the grade, a comment on each stage, the four lists, the overall comment, the notes on the self-evaluation and the analysis. Read; then *Return to trainee* → the confirm → *Returned*.
**Voice:** "Or the whole sheet at once. Copy, and the plan goes out as a brief. Talk the lesson through wherever you like to talk. Paste it back, and every box is filled — every stage, every list, every comment — to be read before it goes anywhere. Return, and the trainee has it."
◆ **Hold on the paste landing.** This is the strongest beat in the film; let the boxes fill and stop.
*This is the exchange, not dictation: the clipboard goes out and comes back. The two must never be cut together as one action.*

## Build notes before filming

- **Everything the film shows is built and live** as of 27 Sep: the hero card, the taught criterion chips and their tip, the two demo courses, the domain. Nothing in the scene list waits on code.
- **Dictation — Chrome or Edge on a computer, always.** Safari, and every browser on iPhone and iPad, defines the engine but cannot dictate with it, so the *Dictate* button hides itself there. Film every dictation beat (scenes 7, 10, 12) in Chrome, microphone permitted beforehand so no permission sheet appears.
- **Dictation and the exchange are two features.** Scene 12 is cursor-in-the-box; scene 13 is Copy → outside → Paste. Cut them as two things, with the voice naming each.
- **Offline for scene 2.** The offline shell (`sw.js`) must have cached the pages once before the Wi-Fi goes off; open each page once online before filming.
- **Drive link for scene 8.** Use a real shared file; the preview needs "anyone with the link".
- **Dictation window for scene 13.** Any model; the brief is what *Copy* puts on the clipboard. If it cannot be shown, cut from *Copy* to *Paste something back* with the voice covering it.
- **Print dialogs.** macOS Safari's dialog is cleanest on screen; export to PDF and show the PDF in Preview for the zooms in scenes 14, 17 and 19.
