ConnectLite For a CELTA center

Teach the course. Lite does the rest.

Connect Lite is the app a CELTA course runs on, from the first plan to the final grade.

Every trainee gets a course of their own. Every tutor gets their evenings back. Built on Cambridge’s handbook, made by a CELTA tutor, and simple enough for anyone.

Questions before you try it

  • Can we use our own course files?

    Yes. The timetable, assignments, observation tasks and input sessions can all be yours. Build the timetable in Lite or read it from your spreadsheet. Link files on your Drive or upload them.

  • Do people need accounts?

    No. Everyone has their own link, which works until the course ends. Turn on PINs and each person sets one the first time. If a tutor link gets out, course admin replaces it in one click and the old copies stop working.

  • Can we take it all away?

    Yes. One button prints every trainee’s full record: each teaching practice with its plan, language analysis and self-evaluation, and every marked assignment. The grades report, the assessor’s pack and the end-of-course reports print separately. Nothing is deleted on a timer.

  • Does it check for plagiarism?

    Yes. At the press of a button, Lite checks an assignment against other work across Lite courses and against the web, and for paraphrase.
