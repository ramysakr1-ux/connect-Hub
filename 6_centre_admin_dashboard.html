Before the course

On, and Connect handles the time before day one: the trainee’s welcome card tells them to check their pre-course task against the answer key, which opens two days before the start, and to pick one of three getting-to-know-you activities for the level they teach. When they run it, and for how long, is read off the timetable — the unassessed teaching slot on day one — unless you say otherwise below. Off, and you paste your own links under Files for trainees.

The candidate agreement

Cambridge requires every centre to give candidates an agreement before the course begins, and says what it must cover: what you expect of them and what happens if it is not met, and what they can expect from you — attendance and illness, refunds, deferrals, plagiarism and its penalties, facilities, special consideration, the tutors’ role, technology, complaints, the course mode, and how their personal information is kept and shared with Cambridge. One box each, in that order. When a trainee signs, they also confirm whether they have a special requirement, the declaration Cambridge asks for in the same agreement. Your trainees read it and sign it on their first visit. You need not start from nothing: each box can take Connect’s wording, written from the Handbook, and the parts in [square brackets] are your centre’s own rules to fill in.

Or your own agreement instead

If your centre already has a candidate agreement as a document, paste its link here and leave the boxes above empty. Your trainees read and sign against that document, and the assessor sees the same link. Leave this empty if you are using the boxes.

Contacts

The tutors’ addresses, for the “Email your tutor” line on the trainees’ home. A trainee sees their group’s tutor first, then the course’s. Nothing shows until an address is here.

Who the volunteer students contact

Who looks after the volunteer students. Their page shows this one name and address under “If you cannot come, tell the centre” — never a tutor’s. Leave it empty and nothing shows.

Files for trainees

Links the trainees read: the input sessions, and anything else. Each becomes a line on their home. What they meet before day one is on the Before the course card above.

Online rooms

Zoom, Meet or Teams links, each a button everyone on the course sees. Name a group and its trainees see that room first. Switch on Assessor for the rooms the assessor will sit in on; they see only those. A room without a link does not show.

Before the assessor’s visit

Come back to this in the last week. The assessor should find everything about the course behind their one link. Most of it is already in Lite: the portfolios, the assignment briefs, the double-marking record, the timetable, the volunteer register and the agreement above. What is left is the date, the files only you have, and which trainees they will watch. A box you leave empty simply does not appear in the pack.
Two or three days before the visit, agree the final timetable with the assessor, submit the centre grade form in Appian, and give them the course notification reference; the pack shows it to them too. On the day, the assessor meets the trainees without tutors present, so tell the trainees beforehand what the assessor is there for.

Who they will watch. Two trainees teaching, for at least an hour and a half, and two portfolios read in full. Tick which; the pack opens with them, and the rest are a click below.

Their names appear here, with a box each, once trainees are on the Roster tab.

Observation tasks

Fifteen sheets the trainees fill in: nine for their own teaching practice group, four for filmed lessons, two for live ones. The words are yours to change; the shape of each sheet is fixed, because it is what the trainee fills in and what prints.

Written assignments

Yours to rewrite: the sections, the fields, the checkboxes a trainee signs, and the criteria it is marked against. The four Cambridge assignments are the four; A5 is the centre's own.
