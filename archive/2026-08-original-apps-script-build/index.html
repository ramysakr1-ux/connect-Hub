Connect Hub

Everything for one teaching practice, in the order you need it. Each form writes a file to your own computer — nothing is sent anywhere until you hand it in yourself.

Before you teach

Trainee

Lesson plan & language analysis

Aims, class profile, anticipated problems, materials and the procedure, with the analysis sheet underneath. Choose a lesson shape and the stages fill themselves in.

After you teach, before you read your feedback

Trainee

Self-evaluation

What went to plan, what didn't, and what you did about last TP's action points. Loads your plan file so you can evaluate against what you actually intended.

While and after watching

Tutor

Teaching practice feedback

Strengths and action points against the assessment criteria, a grade, and comments on the plan, the analysis and the self-evaluation. Assembles the whole TP into one document.

The order matters. Write your self-evaluation before you read your tutor's feedback — that is the point of it. Keep the .celta.json file each form saves: it carries the whole TP from one form to the next, and the copy your tutor returns is what puts your action points into your next lesson plan.
