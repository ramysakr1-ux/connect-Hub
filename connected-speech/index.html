Five features, five activities — then the full picture

activity 1
Elision Trivia Guess the fact8 min

Here's the same phrase written two ways — how it "should" sound, and how it actually sounds when a sound gets dropped. Read the second version out loud, work out the phrase, then guess the real-world fact.

activity 2
The Dictation Computer Puzzle10 min

A dictation computer prints exactly what it hears — and connected speech confuses it. Each sentence on the left has a mistake. Match it to the response on the right that gives you a clue to the real sentence, then say what the computer should have printed.

There's sometimes more than one defensible pairing — that's the nature of the puzzle. Compare your matches with your group.

activity 3
The Link Maze Escape the grid12 min

Your phrase is already waiting in the navy start cell. Say it out loud — which sound joins the two words: /j/, /w/, or /r/? That tells you which direction to click. Land on a new cell, say its phrase, and move again. Reach the red FINISH cell to win.

/j/ → move LEFT /w/ → move UP /r/ → move RIGHT
activity 4
Mark the Links Sherlock Holmes8 min

Click between two words to mark a link (just like drawing a ◡ shape by hand). Read it aloud smoothly, gliding over each link you've marked.

Your marks show in amber. Suggested links (when revealed) show in teal.

activity 5
Spot the Weak Forms Click the words8 min
