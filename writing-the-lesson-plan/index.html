Recap

Aims

  • Name the skill or the language exactly
  • Always give the context
  • Vocabulary aims list the actual items
  • Personal aims come from your last action points

Procedure

  • Bullet points, imperative verbs
  • Write the instruction out, don't summarise it
  • Clear who does what, and for how long
  • A colleague could teach from it
