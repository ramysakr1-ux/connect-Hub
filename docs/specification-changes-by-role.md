# Connect Lite: what changed from the specification

The changes made to Connect Lite from the product specification's recommendation register (R01 to R42), built on 30 and 31 October 2026 and all live. Grouped by who sees each one. The number after each change is its line in the register.

## Course admin and the centre

- **Contact hours from one rule (R01).** The timetable's contact-hours note adds up the sessions by kind from a single rule, shows the sum, and does not count lunch.
- **Who owns which setting (R02).** The timetable says plainly that the course's dates and time zone are set on Course admin, and the days, rooms and sessions on the timetable.
- **Moving a day says what moves first (R03).** Closing or reopening a day lists what will move before anything does: each TP and the assessor's visit with the day it goes to, what waits in Doesn't fit, and that deadlines stay on their dates. Undo puts it back. If the course's start or end date no longer matches the timetable, Course admin says so under the dates.
- **Saved is not the same as ready (R04).** On Roster and links, above the trainees' links, a short Before the links go out list: the course name and dates, the candidate agreement, the timetable published, TP points dealt, assignments set, the trainees on the roster. Each says Ready or Still to do, with a door to where it is done. Advice only; it never stops a link being sent.
- **Input sessions keep their page (R05).** A session placed from the Input library remembers the exact page it came from, so renaming the tile does not break its link.

## Tutors

- **The oldest work first (R17).** On the dashboard, a lesson ready for feedback or an assignment waiting to be marked says how long it has waited, on the course's clock, and the oldest comes first in its group.
- **One group at a time (R18).** The dashboard can show one TP group's rows only, kept on that device. A view, not a permission.
- **The assessor's visit in the day line (R19).** From a week before, the visit appears in the dashboard's line for the day, with the pack one tap away.
- **Who started a feedback draft (R20).** A feedback draft says who started it and who last saved it. It does not lock anyone out.
- **One next action per trainee (R21).** The tracker's trainee strip opens with one next action when there is a reason for it: Stage 3, the Fail warning letter, a withdrawal query, work to mark, Stage 2 or Stage 1.
- **Ask Connect says what it sends (R14).** Beside the Ask Connect button: the lines and the lesson number go through Lite to OpenAI, with no name and no copy kept, and the tutor decides. Nothing reaches the trainee until the feedback is returned.
- **Anonymous feedback stays anonymous (R32).** In the end-of-course feedback, an anonymous answer's comments are held back from the tutors' page, the spreadsheet and the email until at least five trainees have answered (the centre can change the number).
- **The grade meeting is recorded (R31).** The grades report keeps a dated snapshot of every grade at the grade meeting. Any later change is kept beside the trainee with the old grade, the new one, the day and the reason.
- **Letters keep their history (R33).** The Fail warning letter and the academic-integrity warning keep every version issued and every withdrawal with its reason. Staff see the history; the trainee sees only the letter in force.
- **The decision comes before the warning (R34).** The academic-integrity warning cannot be issued until the evidence has been reviewed, the trainee has been spoken to, and who decided and on what basis are recorded.
- **Before the assessor's visit (R40).** The assessor pack opens, for tutors only, a short check: the visit, the trainees chosen, the documents provided, the volunteer register. Never on the assessor's own link or in print.
- **Volunteer hours carried in (R38).** Hours carried over for a volunteer student record where they came from, who entered them and when, and the register shows it.
- **Plain attendance wording (R39).** On the volunteer register, a student who has not replied is shown as likely, with no reply yet, not as expected.

## The TP Point Pool (tutors and the centre)

- **Sets carry their edition and version (R23).** Each set in the Pool shows its version, and the book and edition it was written for.
- **A taken set stays as it was taken (R24).** A course that takes a set keeps that version and says so. When the Pool has a newer version it says that too, and the course's copy changes only if the set is taken again.
- **Offering a set (R25).** Offering a set to the Pool asks for two confirmations: that it is the centre's own work with no publisher's procedure, scans or recordings, and that Connect may edit, ask about, decline or publish it. Both travel with the set, with who confirmed and when.
- **Where an offer has got to (R26).** Once sent, the set shows its status on the course: Sent, Under review, Clarification requested (with the question and where to reply), Published or Declined.

## Trainees

- **The notebook is theirs (R08).** The notebook says it is private and not part of the course record.
- **The plan fills its lesson length (R09).** An empty lesson-length box is filled from the timetable, or from the course's TP points, and says where it came from.
- **Ready to teach, without a score (R10, R11).** The plan's foot strip shows what is in place: main aim, stages, timing against the lesson, materials, and the language analysis as optional. On a phone, where the strip is hidden, a Before you teach card says the same. Nothing is marked or blocked.
- **Last TP's action points, once (R12).** Bringing in last TP's action points again replaces that TP's block instead of adding a second one, and keeps what the trainee wrote under each point.
- **A calm envelope (R15).** Returned feedback arrives in an envelope with no grade on it, and every grade opens the same way: the flap lifts, the sheet rises, the page unfolds. The grade is first seen on the opened page.
- **No hidden word (R28).** The hidden word in assignment briefs is gone; no brief carries it and no marking page looks for it.
- **An AI-use record (R29).** The AI declaration is now a short record: which tool, for what, what was used, and what the trainee checked or changed. A link to the conversation is optional.
- **Who sees what is shared (R36).** The two sharing controls say who will see it: a peer sheet's Connect reaches only that teacher; the plan's share switch reaches the volunteer students, the other trainees and the tutors.

## Everyone who reads the portfolio

- **The official timeline (R35).** Each trainee's portfolio opens with a dated line of the formal events: the candidate agreement, each CELTA 5 stage sent, returned and signed, the final declarations, every letter issued or withdrawn, the grade meeting and any later change, and the handover. Each event opens its document. The trainee, the tutors and the assessor all see it.

## Connect (the owner console)

- **Moving offers on (R26).** In the console, each offered set has a status (Sent, Under review, Ask a question, Published, Declined), and an offer that has moved on no longer counts as waiting.

## Already as recommended

Three lines ask Lite to keep something it already did, and it does:

- **No rewards or rankings in the Pool (R27).**
- **Peer observation private by default (R37),** with sharing chosen and reversible.
- **A warm ending (R41).** No grade and no action points on the trainee's last page.

## Added on 1 November 2026

- **Home hierarchy (R06, trainees).** The home card's title and button are the one main step; under it, at most two quiet lines, with anything that must be signed or put right first, and the rest folded under And N more.
- **Honest locked rooms (R07, trainees).** Already in place since 26 October: a room that is not open yet is not a link, says what opens it, and says so again when pressed.
- **What a star means (R13, tutors and trainees).** Where a tutor stars an action point, and wherever a trainee meets one (the plan, the self-evaluation, the feedback), a line says a starred point carries into the next plan and the next self-evaluation.
- **Earlier TPs by number and date (R16, trainees, tutors, assessor).** Above the earlier records on the feedback page, a chip for each: TP3 · Tue 10 Mar, TP2 · Fri 6 Mar. A chip opens that record.
- **The practice map (R22, tutors).** Trainees already saw one next TP only. Tutors can now print the whole rotation: a table per group, a row per trainee, a column per TP, with the lesson type and the day.
- **The brief behind every attempt (R30, tutors and assessor).** Each submission records which version of the brief it answered; the course keeps every version. The marking page says, for each attempt, whether it answered the brief as it is now or an earlier version, which opens there.
- **Personal keeps (R42, trainees).** The last page's Yours to keep names the three: Download your course card, Your feedback record, Your notebook.

With these, every line of the register is built or already met.
