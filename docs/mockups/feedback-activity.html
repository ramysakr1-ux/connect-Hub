Today’s activity

Steal a strength

Starts from what the peers noticed. Everyone leaves with one thing to try, borrowed from a colleague.

10 minutes
  1. Each observer reads one line from their sheet: something that worked in a colleague’s lesson.
  2. The teacher says what they were thinking at that moment.
  3. Everyone picks one strength to “steal” for their next TP, and says when they’ll use it.
