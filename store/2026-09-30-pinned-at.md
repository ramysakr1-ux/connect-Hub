# Version 49 — a pinned demo course may stamp a record with the moment it happened

The demo clock (v48) makes a course read `settings.demoToday` as today for
ever. Three ops stamp their records with `new Date()` at the store — `post`,
`shareMaterial` and `gridSet` — so a seed planting "a post on day 4" of a
course pinned to day 6 would have written today's real date onto it, and the
stream would have read "30 September" on a course that is in March.

## What changed in `Code.gs`

`pinnedAt_(course, at)`: when the course's settings carry `demoToday` and the
request carries an ISO `at`, that moment is used; otherwise the real clock,
as before. `post`, `shareMaterial` and `gridSet` stamp through it. A real
course cannot be backdated: without the pin, `at` is ignored.

Used by `seed-standing-demos.mjs`.
