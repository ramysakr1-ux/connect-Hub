# 25 Oct 2026 — the notebook (store v129)

Ramy: "We need a smart notebook, connected to the rest of the course"; "share with the whole
course or share with my TP group"; "one for the trainers as well, separate obviously";
"personal: tutors cannot see each other's notebook. They can share but they cannot see each
other's notes"; and the verb: "instead of share with others, say connect with others".

## Kinds
- `notebook` joins TRAINEE_WRITES and TRAINEE_READS, and a new `PRIVATE_KINDS`: `foldRecord_`
  never folds it into a reader's roster, and a reader's `get` of it is refused. A trainee's
  notebook reaches nobody.
- Course kinds `notes` (what trainees connected: a pool keyed by token, items carry `scope`
  group|course and the writer's `group` and `by`) and `tutorNotes` (a pool keyed by the tutor's
  name, lower case; items carry `shared`). Neither goes through putCourse.

## Ops
- `shareNote` {token, item, scope} / `unshareNote` {token, id}: trainee only; upsert by id under
  the lock. The boot answers a trainee with `course.notes`: everyone's course-scope notes and
  their own group's group-scope notes, never their own, with `by`.
- `tutorNotes` {key, who}: that tutor's list plus the notes the others marked shared. Not in the
  boot, so a colleague's browser never caches it. `putTutorNote` {who, item} upserts;
  `dropTutorNote` {who, id}.
- `putNoteAudio` {bytes, type, seconds, token | who}: a voice note into a private folder
  "Notebook recordings (private)" under the course's materials, ≤3 MB; `getNoteAudio` {id}:
  the maker, a tutor's own or a colleague's shared one, a trainee's own or one connected with
  their group or the course; `deleteNoteAudio`: the maker only.

Additive. Live version 136 (v128) checked identical to the repo before the change.

## Later the same day — tutors connect with trainees, trainees reply (store v130, version 138)

Ramy: "can the tutors share notes with trainees and email and whatnot?"; then "they can only reply
to the note, but they cannot initiate a note. They can only send an email."
- `tutorConnectNote` {key, who, item, scope: trainee|group|course, to | group}: the note goes into
  the `notes` pool under "tutor:<name>" with its audience; `tutorUnconnectNote` {who, id} takes it
  back. `sharedNotesFor_` hands a trainee what reaches them (scope trainee = their token), signed.
  `putTutorNote` keeps `sent` (where the tutor connected it) on the tutor's own copy.
- `replyNote` {token, id, text}: a trainee's word back on a tutor's note that reached them; kept on
  the pool copy, capped at 40; the trainee reads only their own reply, the tutor reads them all
  under the note (`tutorNotes` attaches them). Never a note a trainee starts.
- `noteFileFor_`: a trainee hears a tutor's voice note only when it was connected with them.
Verified on the scratch course: connect, seen by the one trainee and not another, reply, read back
by both, a reply from the wrong trainee refused, unconnect. Roles matrix written.

## Later still — a dated pass per person (store v132)

Ramy: "a dated pass per person... when you send the mid-course demo to someone from the console, you pick how
long it lasts, one week by default. I like this idea. Build it." (Demos are sent to individuals only; nothing
public links into one, and only the films are advertised.) The course itself carries no date (only day one, c9,
has `demoExpires`); a link with no pass never expires.
- `mintPass` {owner, course, days 1–60, label}: the owner's; writes {exp, days, label, at} under course kind
  `passes` and answers `{id: "p-<course>-<12 hex>", exp}`. A pass a month past its date is forgotten on the next mint.
- `passInfo` {p}: public; answers `{exp, expired}` so the invitation card can print "opens until <date>".
- Every call that carries `pass` (the browser sends it from the tab's sessionStorage `hub:p`) is checked by
  `passCheck_`: unknown, another course's, or past its date is refused ("This link is not valid." / "This link
  expired on <date>."). Calls without a pass are unaffected. `ping`, `passInfo` and the owner skip the check.
- Soft by design: a pass is a dated invitation, not a lock. The tab keeps it across pages; a link opened fresh (a new
  tab, pasted in) carries none, and links sent before passes existed carry none either. To close those, rotate the keys.
Console: "Links sent from here open for [a day … a month … no date]" on the day-one card, the mid-course card and
Send Lite; Copy, See, WhatsApp, Email and the Send Lite card each mint their own pass for that many days.
