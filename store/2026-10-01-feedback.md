# Feedback thumbs (1 Oct 2026) — version 59

**Why.** Ramy: "is there a way to get some kind of likes feedback and connect
it to Google?" → demos and the offer page only, never a real course.

**What.** A `feedback` tab in the store's spreadsheet: `at | page | course |
role | thumb | text`. Op `feedback {page, course?, role?, thumb:'up'|'down',
text?}` takes NO credential — the widget sits on public and demo pages — and
writes one row (text capped at 400). Op `feedbackSummary` (owner key) returns
this week's `{up, down, comments}`, the total, and the latest ten lines.
`notifyFeedback_()` runs first on the half-hourly timer: new lines since
`FEEDBACK_NOTIFIED` go to the account owner in one mail, with the sheet's
link.

**Client.** `hub-feedback.js` on `offer.html`, `index.html`,
`5_tutor_dashboard.html`: shows only on the offer page or when
`settings.demoToday` is set; thumb, optional line, "Thank you"; remembers the
thumb per page in localStorage. `14_owner.html` reads the summary under the
courses.

**Proved live:** rows from no credential, refusal of a bad thumb, the owner
summary, refusal without the owner key; in a browser the thumbs on the offer
page, a demo home (a tap and a line reached the sheet), a demo dashboard, and
their absence on a real course. Three proof rows remain on the sheet
("Proof row", "UI proof row") — delete by hand if they bother.
