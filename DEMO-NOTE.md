# The note to send with a demo

Paste this under the three links. It is deliberately not the guide — that is
for people using Lite for real. This is five minutes, in an order that ends on
the thing they cannot get anywhere else.

---

Thanks for asking. Three links below — they are a working course, not a video,
so please do type in them. It is a demo course of mine, so nothing you do
touches anybody's real record, and I'll delete it in a day or two.

**Five minutes, in this order:**

**1. Open the tutor link.** Six candidates on a four-week course. Two have work
waiting for you.

**2. Open one of them and return some feedback.** Write a point under
*Strengths in teaching* — then click one of the criterion chips underneath to
tag it, and press the star on an action point. The star is not decoration: it
carries into that candidate's next teaching practice as a priority.

**3. Open the candidate link.** The same feedback from their side, next to the
plan they wrote and their own self-evaluation. That is all a candidate ever
sees — no dashboard, no other candidates.

**4. Open the grades report.** This is the part worth your five minutes. It is
the Cambridge centre grade form: the same fields, the same order, Cambridge's
own wording, so it goes into Appian by paste rather than by retyping. Under
each section open **"Add from the TP records"** — the point you wrote a minute
ago is sitting there, with its criterion code, one click from being in the box.

**5. Open the assessor link.** Everything an assessor needs behind one
view-only link. Try to change something; you can't.

---

**If you want to see it empty instead**, ignore all of the above and start at
the tutor link: add a candidate, send yourself their invitation, and you have
a course. That is genuinely the whole setup.

Any questions, just reply. — Ramy
