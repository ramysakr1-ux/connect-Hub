Task and criteria
The task documents are the candidates' own working templates — each carries the task itself, the assessment criteria it is marked against, the submission requirements and the marking record. Candidates write into them and submit them on Classroom.
Each assignment is 750–1,000 words (CELTA Syllabus and assessment guidelines), and at least two of the four must be written in continuous academic prose. One resubmission is allowed per assignment. Dates are taken from the C/17 course timetable; Assignment 2's resubmission deadline falls on the day of the assessment.
