A general caution, not specific to any site above

A legitimate school pays your salary: it never asks you to pay it for a job, a visa, or a “placement fee” upfront. Get any contract reviewed before signing, and don’t hand over an original passport or degree certificate for someone else to hold: copies only, unless a consulate itself requires the original.
