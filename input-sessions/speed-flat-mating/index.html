1 · Lead-in

The flatmate from hell

On your own: rank these habits from 1 (the worst) to 6 (you could live with it). Then in pairs: compare and explain your top two.

leaves dirty dishes in the sink for days
plays loud music late at night
is always late paying the rent
has friends round every weekend
takes 40-minute showers every morning
eats your food without asking

Discuss: Who do you live with now? Is it easier to live with family, friends or on your own? Why?

2 · Vocabulary

Words you will hear

Match each word in bold to its meaning.

1. My lodger still hasn't paid for last month.
2. You must wear a label to enter this building.
3. I am always seeking new friends wherever I go.
4. I can buddy up with you if you can't afford the rent alone.
5. Choosy people are hard to please.
6. I'm so braindead from working all day.

a. a name tag or badge  ·  b. looking for  ·  c. picky, hard to please  ·  d. too tired to think  ·  e. a paying guest in your home  ·  f. share a room

In pairs, ask and answer:

  1. Have you ever been a lodger, or had one? How was it?
  2. What are you choosy about: food, clothes, friends?
  3. When do you feel braindead?
  4. Who would you buddy up with on a long trip? Why?

B. Useful language. Use these in your questions and answers.

Asking with care

  • Do you mind me asking what you do?
  • Can I ask whether you smoke?
  • Would you say you're a tidy person?
  • How do you feel about having friends round?

Talking about yourself

  • I tend to cook quite late.
  • I'm the kind of person who washes up straight away.
  • I'm not much of a party person.
  • A deal-breaker for me is smoking indoors.

C. Write five questions you will ask at the event.
