Rapport, and how much you talk

45 minutes

Two criteria that tutors write about more than almost any others, and they are the same thing from two sides: what you do with your presence, and what you do with your voice. You have now taught once: this session starts from what that sounded like.

1 · The teacher you remember 2 · Count the talk 3 · Grading 4 · Rapport 5 · Involved at the front 6 · Wrap-up Trainer notes
The teacher you remember 5 minutes

Think of one teacher (any subject, any language) whose classes you actually wanted to be in. In pairs: what did they do? Not what they were like. What did they do.

Two minutes in pairs, three answers to the room. Write them on the board; you will come back to them at the end.

Count the talk 12 minutes

Three minutes of a pre-intermediate lesson. Mark each line, teacher or student, then guess the split by number of words before you check.

%

Grading, not dumbing down 10 minutes

Cambridge 2a: “use comprehensible language… keep your simplified language natural.” Both halves matter, and the second is the one trainees drop.

Rapport, or being liked? 9 minutes

Five things a teacher does. Rapport is not the same as being liked: the test is whether it hands the floor over.

Involved at the front 7 minutes

1d asks for learners “fully involved… during teacher-fronted and learner-centered stages”. Pairwork is the easy half. Five moves for the other one.

Wrap-up 2 minutes

Back to the board. Which of the things your remembered teacher did are on this page? And one sentence each: the one thing you will do differently in your next TP, a rapport move, or a place you will stop talking.
