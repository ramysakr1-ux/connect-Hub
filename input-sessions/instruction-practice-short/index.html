Classroom management: instructions

45 minutesThe longer session

A quick look at what a teacher manages in a classroom, then the part that goes wrong most in a first TP: giving instructions and checking them. Most of the time you practice on each other, in threes. The answers come at the end of each part.

Classroom management in brief 10 minutes

In threes, three minutes: what does a teacher have to manage in a classroom? List as much as you can.

Now answer these six questions together. Then show the answers.

    Fix the instruction 8 minutes

    Five real instructions from first TPs. What goes wrong in each one? Each of you rewrites one or two and says yours out loud to the others. Then show the fixes.

    Good check, or no check? 5 minutes

    The task: “In pairs, read the email and find three things Lisa did last weekend. You have four minutes.” Which of these instruction checking questions (ICQs) really check? Decide for all eight, then check your answers.

    Practice in threes 20 minutes

    Stand up, in threes: one teacher, two students. Three rounds, so everyone is the teacher once.

    1. The teacher picks a task below and has two minutes to write the instruction, word for word, and two ICQs.
    2. The teacher gives it. The students do only what they literally hear, nothing more.
    3. The students tick the list, then say one thing to keep and one to change.
    4. Change roles.
    The students: did the teacher…
      After round three

      Compare yours with a model for each task. There is more than one good way to say it.

      Into TP 2 minutes

      The first task in your next lesson: write the instruction you will say, word for word, and two ICQs. It goes into your plan, under that stage.
