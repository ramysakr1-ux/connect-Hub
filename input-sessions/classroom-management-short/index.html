Classroom management: think on your feet

60 minutesThe longer session

Classroom management is decisions made in real time, under a clock, with five things happening at once. The activities below put trainees in that position directly; nothing here is a worksheet. Correction has a session of its own.

1 · Lead-in 2 · What it covers 3 · Monitoring 4 · Instructions 5 · Stopping an activity 6 · Your internal clock 7 · Wrap-up Trainer notes
Lead-in 4 minutes

What do you think classroom management entails? Four minutes to brainstorm as many things as you can in your group before moving on.

What classroom management entails 15 minutes

The fuller list. Compare it with what your group brainstormed. Click each for a discussion prompt. Four are tagged: one has its own session, three you practice below.

Monitoring 15 minutes

Sixty seconds of a pairwork task. Pairs change as it runs. Go to one and choose what you actually say. You will not get to all of them, which is the point.

0:60

Time. Which pairs did you reach, in which order, and which one never actually needed you?

Instructions 20 minutes

Write the instruction you would actually say, word for word. Then say it out loud to your partner, who does only what you literally asked for.

    The check appears as you type. It is a prompt for discussion, not a grade.

    Stopping an activity

    Five ways to take the room back. Click each for when it is the right one.

    Your internal clock 2 minutes

    Start it, and stop it when you think exactly one minute has passed. No counting, no watching anything.

    Wrap-up 4 minutes

    One thing from the monitoring simulation you will do differently, and one instruction you will write down in full before your next TP rather than improvising it in the room.
