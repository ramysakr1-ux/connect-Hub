A letter home

Trainer notes
Task 1

Ask your teacher

Look at the photos of your teacher's life. Ask your teacher questions.

For the trainees watching The lead-in models the questions the students will ask each other, and it is about me, so they want to ask.
  • Where are you from?
  • What's your job?
  • Where do you live?
  • Who do you live with?
  • What's … like?
Task 2

You and your classmates

Answer for you. Then stand up and ask three classmates.

For the trainees watching Controlled speaking with the same questions, so everyone has the language before the reading.
YouName:Name:Name:
Where are you from?
What's your job?
Where do you live?
Who do you live with?
What's your city like?
Task 3

Words in the letter

Match the words to the meanings.

For the trainees watching Pre-teaching for the reading, meaning only: five words that would block the letter.
1. expensive
2. friendly
3. patient
4. windy
5. lake
  1. nice to other people
  2. with a lot of wind
  3. it costs a lot of money
  4. a big area of water with land all around it
  5. happy to wait and help, not angry
Task 4

A letter from Toronto

Read Kerem's letter quickly (one minute). Which paragraph is about…?

For the trainees watching Reading for gist: one minute, one task. It also shows them that each paragraph has one topic, which matters for their own writing later.

14 Maple Street
Toronto
12 February

Hi Elif,

A How are you and how is everyone at home? I'm writing in English because my teacher says it's good practice!

B I study at a small language school in the centre of Toronto. There are twelve students in my class, from Mexico, Korea, Brazil and Vietnam. Our teacher is Mr Clarke. He's funny and very patient.

C I live with a Canadian family in a big old house near the lake. Mrs Lee is a nurse and her son Daniel is sixteen. They're very kind, but they get up at six every morning!

D Toronto is a beautiful city, but it's very expensive. The buses are fast and clean. It's cold and windy now, but the lake is lovely in winter. I'm very happy here.

Write soon!
Love,
Kerem

his school and class
his home and the family
hello
the city
Task 5

True or false?

Read again. Are the sentences true or false?

For the trainees watching Reading for detail, with a quick check in pairs before feedback.
1. Kerem is from Canada.
2. There are twelve students in his class.
3. His teacher is a woman.
4. He lives with a family.
5. Daniel is a nurse.
6. Toronto is cheap.
7. The buses are fast.
8. Kerem is happy in Toronto.
Task 6

Answer the questions

Write short answers.

For the trainees watching Reading for specific information: short answers, not full sentences.
  1. Where does Kerem study?
  2. Where are the other students from?
  3. What is his teacher like?
  4. How old is Daniel?
  5. What time does the family get up?
  6. What's the weather like?
Task 7

How a letter looks

Match each part of Kerem's letter to its name. Where does each part go?

For the trainees watching Now the text becomes a model for writing: the parts of a letter and where they go.
14 Maple Street, Toronto
12 February
Hi Elif,
Write soon!
Love, Kerem

One paragraph = one topic: hello · school or work · home and people · the city.

Task 8

Plan your letter

You are in a new city. Make notes for each paragraph.

For the trainees watching Planning before writing. At this level, notes for each paragraph make the writing possible.
A · hello
B · school or work
C · home and people
D · the city
Task 9

Write your letter

Write a letter to a friend at home, about 80 words. Use your notes and the parts of a letter.

For the trainees watching The writing itself, then a peer check against a simple list. I monitor and note common errors for the end.

Then swap with a partner and check: ☐ address and date  ☐ Hi … ,  ☐ four paragraphs  ☐ Write soon! Love, …
