Trainer notes
Task 1

Three journeys

In pairs, look at the photos. What transport are the people using? Where do you think they are going?

For the trainees watching The photos set the scene and get students guessing before they read anything.
Task 2

Six words

Match the words to the meanings. Then guess: how is each word connected to the journeys?

For the trainees watching Pre-teaching, meaning only: these six words would block the texts. Then a prediction, so they read to check.
1. cow
2. rabbit
3. fence
4. oxygen
5. crash
6. experiment

  2. to hit something hard while you are moving
  3. the gas in the air that we need to breathe
  4. a wooden or wire structure around land
  5. a small animal with long ears that digs holes
  6. a large farm animal kept for milk and meat
Task 3

Read your text

You are A, B or C. Read only your text: quickly first (2 minutes). Were your guesses right? Then read again and answer your questions.

For the trainees watching A jigsaw: each student reads a different text, first quickly for their predictions, then again for detail.

Task 5

Talk about it

In your group, discuss.

For the trainees watching A short personal response before the grammar.
  1. Which journey was the most amazing? Why?
  2. Which journey was the most difficult? Why?
  3. All three became films. Which would you most like to watch?
Task 6

While they were travelling…

Read the sentences about the journeys. Answer the questions with a partner.

For the trainees watching The grammar comes from the stories they now know. Meaning first, with concept questions, then the form box.
  1. While the astronauts were travelling to the moon, there was an explosion.
  2. While Ernesto was travelling, he met many poor people.
  3. The girls were walking through the desert when they saw the fence.
1. In sentence 1, which started first: the travelling or the explosion?
2. Which action was longer?
3. Was the travelling finished when the explosion happened?
4. Which tense is the longer action?
5. Which tense is the shorter action?

Longer action, in progress: was / were + verb-ing   they were travelling

Shorter action, finished: past simple   something went wrong

while + the longer action  ·  when + either

Task 7

Past continuous or past simple?

Put the verbs in the right form.

For the trainees watching Controlled practice with the same stories, so the meaning is already clear and only the form is new.
  1. While the astronauts (travel) to the moon, an explosion (destroy) important equipment.
  2. Ernesto (study) medicine when he (decide) to travel across Latin America.
  3. While Ernesto and Alberto (ride) through South America, they (crash) into a cow.
  4. When it (rain) one night, the three girls (escape) from the camp.
  5. While the girls (walk) through the desert, they (see) the fence.
  6. The whole world (watch) on TV when the astronauts (land) safely in the ocean.
Task 8

Your own journey

Think of a journey you remember: a holiday, a move, or a trip that went wrong. Make notes, then tell your group. Use while or when + past continuous at least twice. Whose journey was the most interesting?

For the trainees watching The freer stage: their own journeys, with the target language. I monitor and note errors for the end.
