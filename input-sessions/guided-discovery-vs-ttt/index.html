What just happened? With your partner

You experienced two approaches to teaching grammar. The first lesson focused on one use of the past continuous and the other focused on different uses of the present continuous. However, the grammar focus is largely irrelevant as nearly any grammar point could have been chosen for either approach. Bearing this in mind, with your partner discuss the following questions.

Remember! We can only adopt a guided discovery or a TTT approach when we are teaching grammar or possibly lexis. We cannot adopt this approach when teaching writing or speaking etc. They are only for grammar or lexis.
