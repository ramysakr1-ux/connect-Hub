Sounds vs letters 8 minutes

Listen, write the word, then count its letters and its sounds. They are not the same number.

Why doesn’t the number of letters match the number of sounds?
Click to reveal
Because English spelling records history, not pronunciation. “letter” has six letters and four or five sounds depending on whether you pronounce the r; “phoneme” has seven letters and five sounds. A phoneme is a unit of sound, and the alphabet was never built to write one.
