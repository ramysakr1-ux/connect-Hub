Giving feedback on tasks

45 minutes

Not error correction; this is feedback on whether students completed the task itself: did they get the right answer, finish the discussion, reach a decision. Skipping this stage means students never find out if the activity actually worked.

Lead-in 5 minutes

Think of the last TP you watched. After a task finished, how did the teacher check it, and how long did it take? In pairs: was there a moment where students never found out whether they were right?

Two minutes in pairs, then three answers from the room. No feedback from the trainer yet: the session supplies it.

Match the task to its feedback method 10 minutes

Every task type needs a different way of checking answers. Click each task for the fastest, clearest way to close the loop.

Spot the weak feedback move 13 minutes

Same task, five ways a teacher might run feedback on it. Pick each one to find out whether it works.

Run it live 8 minutes

In threes: one teacher, two students. The students do the task for 90 seconds, then the teacher runs feedback in under a minute, method matched to the task. Swap roles. Click a card to take it.

Wrap-up 2 minutes

One line each, round the room: the feedback method you will use in your next TP, and for which task.
