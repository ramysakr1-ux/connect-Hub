The key

On the page: the acronym matches and the scenarios (YLE; TOEFL iBT; PTE Academic; B2 First; IELTS).

Watch for

  • Trainees who want a lesson on how to teach each exam. This session is a map, not the territory; say so at the start.
  • Exam facts quoted as permanent. Everything here was right when written; the official pages win.

Sources. Exam facts are from Cambridge English, IELTS, ETS and Pearson's own published descriptions of each exam.
