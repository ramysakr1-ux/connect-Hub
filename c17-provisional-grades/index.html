Two headings follow Cambridge's own field names (Administration Handbook June 2025, p43), so a section pastes straight into Appian: Update on strengths and areas for development — a required field for every candidate, withdrawn ones included — and What evidence was provided for a Pass / Higher Grade, which covers a borderline Pass/Fail → Pass, not just an upgrade.
§11.6. A candidate who fails one written assignment may still be recommended a Pass or a Pass B, where there is sufficient evidence of that assignment's assessment criteria in their record of teaching practice and/or their written work as a whole — the assessor documents this in the course report. They are not eligible for Pass A. A candidate who fails more than one assignment is not eligible for a Pass. p43: complete the evidence sections for any candidate who was borderline, so the reason for the final grade is on record.
