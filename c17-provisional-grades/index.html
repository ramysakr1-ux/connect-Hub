Every heading here is a field on the Cambridge form, in the same order, so a section pastes straight across: strengths and areas for development in planning and in teaching, then Evidence needed for a Pass / Higher Grade (if applicable), which appears only on a borderline grade — Pass/Fail, Pass B/Pass, Pass A/Pass B. There is no update on strengths field on the provisional form; that one belongs to the final grade.
§11.6. A candidate who fails one written assignment may still be recommended a Pass or a Pass B, where there is sufficient evidence of that assignment's assessment criteria in their record of teaching practice and/or their written work as a whole — the assessor documents this in the course report. They are not eligible for Pass A. A candidate who fails more than one assignment is not eligible for a Pass. p43: complete the evidence field for any candidate who was borderline, so the reason for the final grade is on record.
